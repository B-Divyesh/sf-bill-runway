# Bill Runway copy audit

Audited 2026-08-30 for polish round 2. This is the complete
sentence inventory for visible product, state, legal, offline, 404, and README
prose. Repeated header labels, footer text, and standalone/legal SPA fallback
copy are listed once with every route where they appear. Commands, form labels,
names, amounts, dates, and navigation labels are not sentences; they are
checked for consistent terminology below. Hyphenated words count as one word.
No audited sentence exceeds 22 words or contains a banned marketing word.
